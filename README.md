Tepeyollotl is short video game produced in 4 months at ENJMIN (French National School of Games and Interactive Digital Medias). We wanted to share our experience and our code with others.

Tepeyollotl is a student work. The assignment we had was the following: "Create an interactive experience that a random person could understand and go through in ten minutes. You can't provide any explanation to the player".

We're still deciding what we are going to do with the assets of the game, so for the moment it's the code alone.

To compile the source code, you need libnds, libfat and the whole devkitARM r21 (https://devkitpro.org/). Newer versions of devkitARM will break the code.
